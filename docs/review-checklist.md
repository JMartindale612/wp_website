# Website review checklist

Use this before committing a substantial website change.

## Desktop and mobile

- Review Home, Project, People, Contact, News, Privacy, Thank you, and each News post.
- Review at a narrow mobile width and at 200% browser zoom.
- Confirm header navigation, footer links, partner logos, cards, and advisory-group table remain readable.
- Confirm no horizontal scrolling appears except where deliberately required.

## Keyboard and accessibility

- Use `Tab` from the top of the page: the Skip to main content link should appear first.
- Open About with the keyboard and tab through Project, People, and Contact.
- Check visible focus on links, buttons, and form fields.
- Confirm images either have meaningful alternative text or are intentionally decorative.
- If reduced motion is enabled in the operating system, confirm the page does not animate unexpectedly.

## Forms

- Confirm the contact and newsletter forms remain separate.
- Test required-field feedback only; do not send a test submission unless you intend to create a real Formspree entry.
- Confirm the privacy notice and unsubscribe information are visible.
- After a real submission, check the configured Formspree recipient and thank-you redirect.
