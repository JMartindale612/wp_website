Privacy notice

Information submitted through this website

The contact form collects your name, email address, optional organisation, and message so that the Weaponised Pasts project team can respond to your enquiry. The newsletter form collects your email address so that the team can send you project news and updates.

How submissions are handled

Both forms are delivered through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service. Formspree processes and may store the information you submit. Please do not send sensitive personal information through these forms. You can read Formspree's privacy policy for details of its processing and storage practices.

Your choices

You can ask the project team to correct or delete information you have submitted. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe from future updates at any time. To make a request or unsubscribe, email {{ site.contact_email }}.
